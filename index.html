Secret input
Weave
How to perform
  1. Open the page. Let the spectator pinch, spin and Reweave the threads; it's just a pretty, random weave.
  2. Have a card chosen and shown to you.
  3. Pick the phone back up and "adjust the weave": spread/pinch steps the value (A → 2 → … → K → A), twist a quarter turn steps the suit. Each step gives a tick and updates the indicator in the bottom‑right corner.
  4. Let go: the image straightens and the hidden message is rewoven to match.
  5. Tilt the phone and look along it from the charger end: the card you chose was. Turn the phone a quarter turn and look from the left edge: the seven of hearts.

Other controls: one finger pans, double‑tap recentres (keeps the card). Long‑press the loom wordmark to open this panel. On a computer: scroll = value, shift+scroll = suit, arrow keys step value/suit, S opens setup.

Tip: add the page to your home screen so it opens full screen without browser bars.
